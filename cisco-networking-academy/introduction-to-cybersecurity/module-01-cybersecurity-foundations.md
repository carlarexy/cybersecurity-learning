## 🌐 1. The World of Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, devices, applications, and data from unauthorized access, disruption, damage, or misuse.

As more activities move into digital environments, individuals, businesses, governments, and other organizations increasingly depend on technology.

This creates a larger attack surface for cybercriminals and other threat actors.

Cybersecurity therefore plays an important role in protecting:

- Personal information
- Financial information
- Business data
- Communication systems
- Network infrastructure
- Applications and services
- Critical infrastructure

### Why Cybersecurity Matters

A successful cyberattack can have consequences that go beyond a compromised computer.

Depending on the target and type of attack, the impact can include:

- Loss of sensitive information
- Financial losses
- Disruption of services
- Damage to an organization's reputation
- Loss of customer trust
- Privacy violations
- Operational disruption

This reinforced for me that cybersecurity is not only a technical issue. It can directly affect individuals, businesses, governments, and society.

## 🗂️ 2. Organizational Data

Organizations collect, store, process, and transmit large amounts of information.

Examples of organizational data include:

| Type of Data | Examples |
|---|---|
| Customer Data | Names, contact details, account information |
| Financial Data | Payment information, transactions, financial records |
| Employee Data | Personnel records, payroll information, identification details |
| Business Data | Reports, plans, contracts, internal documents |
| Authentication Data | Usernames, passwords, credentials, access information |
| Operational Data | System information, processes, logs, technical information |

Not all information has the same level of sensitivity, but compromised data can create serious consequences for an organization.

### Why Attackers Want Organizational Data

Cybercriminals may target organizational information because it can be used for:

- Financial gain
- Identity theft
- Fraud
- Extortion
- Espionage
- Further attacks
- Selling information to other criminals

This helped me understand why data is considered an important asset that organizations need to protect.

---

## 🔐 3. The Importance of Protecting Data

Protecting data is a fundamental part of cybersecurity.

Organizations need to make sure that information is:

- Accessible to authorized users
- Protected from unauthorized access
- Protected from unauthorized modification
- Available when it is needed

This connects with the three fundamental security principles commonly known as the **CIA Triad**:

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confidentiality | Information should only be accessible to authorized individuals or systems |
| Integrity | Information should remain accurate and protected from unauthorized modification |
| Availability | Systems and information should remain accessible when required |

Understanding these three principles gives me a useful framework for thinking about security.

For example, an attack that steals confidential information primarily affects **confidentiality**, while an attack that alters records affects **integrity**. An attack that makes a service unavailable affects **availability**.

## 👤 4. Cyber Attackers

A cyber attacker is an individual or group that attempts to gain unauthorized access to systems, networks, devices, or information.

Attackers can have different levels of technical capability, motivations, and resources.

Their objectives may include:

- Stealing information
- Making money
- Disrupting services
- Gaining unauthorized access
- Spying on individuals or organizations
- Damaging systems
- Supporting political or strategic objectives

This reminded me that there is no single type of attacker or single reason behind a cyberattack.

---

## 🧩 5. Attacker Motivation

Different attackers may have different motivations.

| Motivation | Possible Objective |
|---|---|
| Financial Gain | Stealing money, credentials, or valuable information |
| Information Theft | Obtaining sensitive personal or organizational data |
| Espionage | Gathering information for strategic or intelligence purposes |
| Disruption | Interrupting systems or services |
| Ideology | Supporting a political, social, or ideological cause |
| Revenge | Targeting an individual or organization because of a personal dispute |
| Strategic Objectives | Achieving broader political, military, or organizational goals |

Understanding attacker motivation can be useful when assessing potential threats because the attacker's objective can influence what they target and how they operate.

## 🌍 6. Cyberwarfare

The module also introduced the concept of **cyberwarfare**.

Cyberwarfare refers to the use of cyber capabilities in conflicts involving nations or other organized entities.

Cyber operations can potentially target:

- Government systems
- Military systems
- Communication infrastructure
- Energy infrastructure
- Transportation systems
- Financial systems
- Other critical infrastructure

This showed me that cybersecurity extends beyond protecting individual computers or company networks.

At a national level, cybersecurity can also involve protecting critical systems that society depends on.
