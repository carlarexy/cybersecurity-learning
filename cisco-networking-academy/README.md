This folder documents my learning journey through **Cisco Networking Academy**, where I am strengthening my computer networking and cybersecurity knowledge through structured online training.

## 🧭 Learning Approach

I am treating networking and cybersecurity as connected disciplines.

For each topic, I want to understand:

> **What is it?**
>
> **How does it work?**
>
> **Why does it matter?**
>
> **What can go wrong?**
>
> **How can it be exploited?**
>
> **How can it be detected?**
>
> **How can it be protected?**
>
> **How can I demonstrate that I understand it?**

This approach helps me move beyond memorizing concepts and toward understanding how they apply in practical cybersecurity environments.
