## 🧠 Learning Approach

I am using TryHackMe as a practical complement to my other cybersecurity learning.

Rather than only memorizing concepts, I want to understand how they are applied in realistic situations.

For each topic, I aim to ask:

> **What is happening?**
>
> **Why is it happening?**
>
> **What evidence can I find?**
>
> **Is the activity legitimate or malicious?**
>
> **What should happen next?**
>
> **How can the situation be detected or prevented?**
